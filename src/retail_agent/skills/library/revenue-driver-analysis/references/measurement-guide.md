# Measurements and permissible interpretations

| Question | Measurements needed | Important limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which product earned more? | Same-period qualifying revenue at product grain | The difference alone does not explain why. |
| Which reached more buyers? | Distinct customers for each product | These counts do not measure units or orders. |
| Did buyers spend more on this product? | Revenue and distinct customers for that product and period | Revenue per customer can rise through price, quantity or purchase frequency. |
| Was realized unit price higher? | Revenue and matching units, or actual unit sale prices | Product mix affects aggregate averages; catalogue price is a different measure. |
| Did volume increase? | Matching unit counts for comparable periods | A distinct-customer increase does not prove a unit increase. |
| How concentrated was revenue? | Selected-population revenue and matching population total | The total must have the same scope, period and qualifying-status rules. |

These are methodological examples, not current facts or required tool sequences. Use current authorized evidence for any numerical answer.
