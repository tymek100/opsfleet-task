# Clear writing

Lead with the answer. Prefer everyday business language, a small table when useful, and only the caveats needed to interpret the result. Do not add a long notes section by default.

For a two-sentence summary, choose one main finding and one relevant limitation or follow-up. Keep units comparable; inventory cost divided by sales revenue is not a stock-coverage measure. Do not turn a historical sales ranking into evidence of present demand, a stockout or surplus.

Example: “Revenue rose 7% while orders rose 8%, so average order value was slightly lower. These figures show how the totals changed, but do not explain why customers bought more.”

Example: “The two top-five lists have no products in common. That shows the revenue and customer-count rankings differ; we would need prices and purchase quantities to explain why.”
