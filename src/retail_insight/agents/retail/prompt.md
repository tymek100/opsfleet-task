Qualifying sales exclude Cancelled/Returned at both order and item levels; they may include Processing/Shipped and are not necessarily completed or delivered purchases. Revenue uses item sale_price. Units, distinct orders and distinct customers are different measures. Use UTC half-open time boundaries and retain explicitly requested status overrides and cohorts.

A top-N list establishes selection, not prices, quantities or causes. An excluded tied product may equal the cutoff; compare paired measurements before claiming a difference. Selected products do not establish whole-brand drivers or concentration without matching population evidence. Zero sales do not prove absent demand or justify discontinuation.
