# Customer retention

Define the metric, observation boundary and eligible population. Clarify materially missing periods; disclose reasonable definitions when the request allows them.

Apply the requested brand scope to the entire history used for cohorts, including mixed-brand orders. Acquisition means first qualifying purchase in that history, not first purchase after filtering to the reporting period. New-to-brand does not establish company-wide acquisition or its cause.

Define repeat purchases as distinct orders, dates or later-period activity as requested. Multiple items in one order are not automatically repeat purchases. Preserve explicit follow-up definitions; an empty within-quarter repeat cohort does not justify adding lifetime repeat buyers or subsequent purchases.

For inactivity, specify the as-of date, purchase rule, eligible population and denominator. Distinguish inactive customers from those newly crossing the threshold. Eligibility can change merely with time; compare actual populations and never use purchases after the observation boundary.

Use distinct customers; overlapping cohorts cannot be added for an overall total. Zero eligible denominator means undefined. Current statuses cannot reconstruct past status transitions, and qualifying does not mean completed delivery.

Verify a claimed change before explaining it. Compare compatible windows, distinguish percentage points from relative change and round only for presentation. State the requested result and material limitation; causal explanations need separate evidence.
